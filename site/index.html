How to read the dashboard

Entry timing first

A relevant event within three calendar days triggers a wait-and-reassess signal for the next NYSE session after that event cluster. Entering now earns premium sooner but crosses the catalyst. Waiting reduces event uncertainty; price may move away and implied volatility may fall, reducing premium.

A historical strike screen

Five years of adjusted daily closes are used to measure the lowest subsequent closing price over comparable trading-session windows. The fifth percentile becomes a model price ceiling. The app screens actual regular puts below that ceiling and 1% below the reference close, with positive bids, open interest ≥25, and relative bid/ask spread ≤50%.

What the numbers mean

Collateral = strike ×100. Indicative credit = bid ×100. Breakeven = strike − bid. Return = bid ÷ strike, before fees and tax. Worst historical closing decline is context, not a maximum possible loss. Overlapping samples are dependent; overnight gaps and intraday lows can exceed this screen.

Coverage and limitations

Macro coverage includes BLS inflation, jobs and labor releases, BEA GDP/PCE/trade, and scheduled FOMC decisions. Earnings cover all rows returned by the provider for the next 35 days; future unannounced reports may be absent. ETF earnings risk uses selected company proxies, not every holding. IWM constituent earnings are not modeled. Unscheduled news, Treasury auctions, and all Fed speeches are not covered.

SOXL deserves extra care

SOXL targets three times daily semiconductor returns. Compounding and volatility can produce large losses. Assignment commits you to owning the shares, and a later covered call cannot guarantee recovery. The app never places orders.

Data freshness

The refresh timestamp is collection time, not a real-time quote timestamp. Bid timestamps are not supplied by the public options feed; last trade time is separate. Snapshots older than 12 hours are flagged. Missing calendars block an all-clear signal. GitHub cron may run late or be disabled after extended repository inactivity.
